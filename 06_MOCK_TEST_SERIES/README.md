### Question Typologies & Cognitive Benchmark
The papers feature four distinct question topologies matching the most rigorous competitive standards (GATE XH-C4 and UGC NET Paper II):
1. **Format A: Rigorous 4-Option Conceptual MCQs** with razor-sharp doctrinal distractors.
2. **Format B: Assertion (A) and Reason (R) Evaluative Dyads**, requiring candidates to determine whether (R) is the true causal/philosophical explanation of (A).
3. **Format C: Multi-Statement Questions (MSQ Style)**, evaluating combinations of Statements I, II, III, and IV.
4. **Format D: Systematic Doctrinal & Textual Match List-I with List-II Tables**.

### Structure of Each Mock Paper File
Each mock test file is organized into three distinct, self-contained sections:
- **Section I: Complete Question Paper (Q.1 to Q.100)**: Formatted for clean examination simulation without revealing answers.
- **Section II: Master Answer Key Table (Q.1 to Q.100)**: Quick-reference grading table mapping Question Number, Correct Key, Curriculum Domain, and Format Type.
- **Section III: Exhaustive Solutions, Foundational Textual Citations, and Comprehensive Distractor Diagnosis**:
  - *Correct Option* identified clearly.
  - *Primary Textual Reference* citing classic treatises (e.g., *Tattvacintamani*, *Slokavarttika*, *Sribhasya*, *Mulamadhyamakakarika*, *Critique of Pure Reason*, *Theaetetus*, *Being and Time*, *Naming and Necessity*, etc.).
  - *Philosophical Grounding & Demonstration* providing rigorous multi-step derivations.
  - *Comprehensive Distractor Diagnosis* explicitly breaking down each wrong choice and demonstrating what fallacy it commits, why it is erroneous, or which rival school propounds it.
