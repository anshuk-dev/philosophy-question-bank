### Features of the Web Application:
- **Full-Length Mock Test Simulator**:
  - Mock Paper 01 & Mock Paper 02 (100 Questions each).
  - 180-minute countdown exam timer.
  - Authentic exam question palette (Answered, Unanswered, Marked for Review).
  - Instant scoring calculation (+2.00 for correct, -0.67 negative marking).
  - Question-by-question review with primary textual citations and full distractor analyses.
- **Topic-Wise Practice & Interview Deck (213 Questions)**:
  - Instant feedback on click (Green for correct, Red for incorrect).
  - Complete derivation and option-by-option diagnosis for all 4 choices.
  - Filter by 5 major domains and 17 subtopics.
- **Deep Concept Search Engine**:
  - Live search across all 413 questions, groundings, and distractor refutations.
  - Search by philosopher (Gangeśa, Kant, Kripke, Dasein, Ambedkar, etc.).
- **Mobile-Responsive & Dark Mode**:
  - Touch-friendly responsive layout optimized for mobile screens.
  - Dark / Light mode toggle with saved preferences.
  - LaTeX mathematical logic rendered smoothly via KaTeX ($\forall x, \exists y, \to, \lor, \land$).

## 🎯 Rigorous Verification Standard
Every question in this bank adheres to:
1. **Zero Hallucination**: 100% backed by classical and contemporary primary literature (*Tattvacintāmaṇi*, *Ślokavārttika*, *Brahmasūtrabhāṣya*, *Critique of Pure Reason*, *Philosophical Investigations*, *Naming and Necessity*, etc.).
2. **Exhaustive Distractor Diagnosis**: Every single incorrect option (A, B, C, D) has an explicit philosophical explanation explaining why it fails or which rival school it represents.
3. **No Trivial Recall**: Tests boundary conditions, inner tensions, and subtle conceptual distinctions worthy of an Assistant Professor faculty interview.
