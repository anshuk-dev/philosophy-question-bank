## 2. Verification Standards & Audit Protocol

Each authored module was audited against four strict academic criteria established in the Swarm Blueprint:

1. **Zero Hallucination & Textual Authenticity**:
   - Every Sanskrit technical term (e.g., *sādhya*, *avacchedaka*, *pratiyogin*, *sāmānyalakṣaṇa-sannikarṣa*, *anupalabdhi*, *arthāpatti*, *khyātivāda*, *apṛthaksiddhi*, *vivartavāda*, *apūrva*), Greek term (*to eon*, *ousia*, *eidos*, *energeia*, *dynamis*, *akrasia*, *noesis*, *dianoia*), Latin scholastic formulation (*privatio boni*, *analogia entis*, *per se* vs *per accidens*, *aliquid quo nihil maius cogitari possit*), and German phenomenological/idealist technical vocabulary (*Kopernikanische Wende*, *transzendentale Apperzeption*, *Schematismus*, *epoché*, *Zuhandenheit*, *Vorhandenheit*, *Zeugganzheit*, *Gestell*, *Horizontverschmelzung*) was verified against authentic primary texts.
2. **IIT & Central University Assistant Professor Rigor**:
   - No simple rote-recall or superficial factual questions. Every question tests subtle boundary conditions, inner tensions, systemic counter-arguments, and cross-school dialectics.
3. **Exhaustive Distractor Diagnosis**:
   - For every question across all formats (MCQ, Assertion-Reason, MSQ, Matching), all four choices (A, B, C, D) are evaluated. Every incorrect distractor has an explicit philosophical diagnosis explaining *why* it fails or identifying which rival school/fallacy it represents.
4. **Unambiguous Determinacy**:
   - Verified that exactly one defensible correct answer exists under the specified philosophical context.
